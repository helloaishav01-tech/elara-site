## 🎯 The Boutique-Brand Playbook (PATH 2 — detailed)

This is what you said you want. Here's exactly how to do it.

### Step 1 — Identify your *target customer*

Not all shoe brands. You want:
- **Small to mid Indian / global boutique brands** with weak websites
- 1k–50k Instagram followers (big enough to afford you, small enough to need help)
- Currently selling on Instagram DMs or a basic Shopify
- Aesthetic-conscious — bridal, occasion wear, slow fashion, indie luxury

**Where to find 50 of them in 1 hour:**
- Instagram search: `#indianbridalfootwear`, `#kolhapuri`, `#mojaris`, `#handcraftedshoes`, `#bridalheels`
- Etsy categories: Women's shoes → handmade
- Google Maps: \"boutique footwear store [your city]\" — visit + DM owner
- Indian D2C: BIBA, House of Misu, FizzyGoblet, Pasha India (study them, not pitch them)

### Step 2 — Build your 2-piece pitch kit

**(a) A Loom video (3–4 min, free)** showing:
1. ELARA homepage with petals + cursor + brand strip — 30 sec
2. Click through Collections → Products → Quick View — 45 sec
3. Add to cart → Checkout → Confirmation email arriving in real inbox — 60 sec
4. Admin dashboard — orders + coupons — 30 sec
5. Mobile responsiveness (resize browser or actual phone) — 20 sec
6. \"I can re-skin this for your brand in 7 days for ₹40,000 / $500\" — 20 sec

**(b) A 1-page PDF** with:
- 4 screenshots of ELARA
- The list of features (use bullets from `README.md`)
- 3 testimonials *from people who've seen the site* (friends, professors — phrase them honestly: \"Aisha built this from scratch — incredible eye for design\" — Friend's Name, role)
- Your price + 7-day delivery promise
- Contact info

### Step 3 — The cold DM template that converts

> *Subject (Instagram DM):*
>
> Hi [Brand Name] 🌸 I've been admiring your [specific product — e.g. \"ivory bridal mojaris\"] for weeks.
>
> Quick — I noticed your customers currently order through DMs. I built a luxury e-commerce site called **ELARA** specifically for boutique footwear brands like yours, with cart, checkout, payment, email confirmations + admin dashboard.
>
> Can I send you a 3-min walkthrough? I can re-skin it in your brand's name + colors in **7 days** for a flat fee. No subscription, you own the code.
>
> Either way — your [latest collection / hero pair] is stunning. 🌷
>
> — Aisha

**Rules:**
- Send to **10 brands per day**, **5 days a week** = 50 leads / week
- Expect **2–5% reply rate** = **1–3 conversations / week**
- Expect **1 sale per 15–25 conversations** = **1 sale every 2–4 weeks**
- At $500/sale, that's **$500–$1,000 / month** within 2 months of consistent outreach

### Step 4 — The delivery process (when someone says yes)

1. **30-min discovery call** — get logo, colors, 10–30 product photos, brand voice
2. **Day 1–2:** swap palette in `tailwind.config.js`, swap fonts in `index.html`, replace product data in `data/products.js`
3. **Day 3–4:** rewrite copy on Home / About / Editorial pages with their voice
4. **Day 5:** deploy to Vercel under their domain
5. **Day 6:** train them on the admin panel via Zoom
6. **Day 7:** hand off + invoice

**Charge 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.** Use Razorpay / Stripe payment links.

### Step 5 — Upsell to retainer

After delivery, offer:
> *\"Want me to handle hosting + small changes + monthly product additions? ₹3,000 / month — covers everything.\"*

3 retainer clients = ₹9,000 / month passive income on top of new sales.
